🟡 Privilege Escalation

📌 Description

After gaining access, the attacker obtained elevated privileges within the system.

🧠 Behavior Observed
Assignment of special privileges
Elevated session creation

🔍 Detection
event.code:4672

📊 Indicators
Privileged logon session
Administrative capabilities

🧩 MITRE ATT&CK
T1078 — Valid Accounts (abuse of privileges)

🚨 Analyst Note

Privilege escalation significantly increases the attacker’s capabilities within the system.
